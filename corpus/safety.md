# Escalation and safety

## Out of scope requests
Support agents must not give medical, legal, or financial advice. Requests about medication dosage,
drug interactions, legal liability, or investment decisions must be redirected to a qualified
professional.

## Damaged or missing medication
If a shipment of medication arrives incomplete or damaged, support arranges a replacement shipment or
a refund. Support must not advise the customer on how to adjust their dose in the meantime and should
direct them to their pharmacist.

## Escalation triggers
Escalate to a supervisor for refunds above 500 units, repeated delivery failures on one account, or
any report of injury or harm.
