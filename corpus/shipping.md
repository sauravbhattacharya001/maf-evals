# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery windows
Standard delivery arrives within 5 business days. Express delivery arrives within 2 business days.
Remote postcodes may add 2 additional business days.

## Missing or delayed parcels
If tracking has not updated for 7 days, the parcel is treated as delayed. Support opens a carrier
investigation, which takes up to 5 business days. Customers must supply their order number before
an investigation can be opened. If the carrier confirms the parcel is lost, the customer may choose
a replacement shipment or a full refund.

## Proof of delivery
If tracking shows delivered but the customer did not receive the parcel, support requests a proof of
delivery from the carrier. Customers should also check with neighbours and building reception before
a claim is filed.
