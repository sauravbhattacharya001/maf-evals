# Refunds and billing

## Duplicate charges
A duplicate charge happens when a payment is authorised twice for one order. Pending authorisations
usually clear on their own within 5 business days. If both charges have posted, support refunds the
extra charge. The customer must supply their order number and both transaction identifiers.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Card refunds take 5 to 10 business days
to appear, depending on the issuing bank. Support cannot guarantee a specific arrival date.

## Refund limits
Agents may issue refunds up to 500 units without escalation. Refunds above that amount require a
supervisor. Refunds are only issued for damaged goods, duplicate charges, or undelivered orders.
