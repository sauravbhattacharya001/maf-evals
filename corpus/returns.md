# Returns

## Return window
Most items may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Perishable goods and personalised items
cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged.

## Damaged items
If an item arrives damaged, the customer should photograph the damage and the packaging, then open a
return request from their account. Photographs let support approve the return without an inspection.
Damaged item returns are free of charge.

## Return shipping
Standard returns use a prepaid label available in the account area. Refunds are processed once the
warehouse receives and inspects the item, usually within 3 business days of arrival.
