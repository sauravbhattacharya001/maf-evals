# Subscriptions

## Cancelling a subscription
Customers can cancel from Account, then Subscriptions, then Cancel plan. Cancellation must be
completed at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next billing cycle.

## Refunds after renewal
If a renewal was charged within the last 14 days and the plan was not used, support may refund the
renewal. Usage is measured by logins and feature activity.

## Pausing instead of cancelling
Subscriptions can be paused for up to 3 months. Paused plans keep customer data and do not bill.
